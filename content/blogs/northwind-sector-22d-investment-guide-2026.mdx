## Three Demand Drivers

<a id="drivers"></a>

**The airport is no longer a plan.** Commercial flights began on 15 June 2026 with a
Phase 1 capacity of 12 million passengers a year, scaling to 70 million by Phase 4.
Airports of this size create hotel, MRO, cargo and logistics employment within a
15–20 km radius, and the residential demand that follows is largely rental first,
then end-user.

**Film City is being built next door.** Phase 1 on 230 acres is being developed by
Bayview Bhutani Film City (Boney Kapoor with Bhutani Infra) with about ₹1,510 crore of
investment, and the first permanent studio is targeted by end-2026. Studios employ
crews, technicians and support staff on long shoots — exactly the tenant profile that
prefers a furnished 3 BHK on a short lease.

**The industrial belt is filling in.** The Medical Devices Park in Sector 28,
electronics and semiconductor clusters, and the Toy and Apparel Parks are all being
developed in the corridor around Sector 22D.

## Why Northwind Chose Sector 22D

<a id="why-northwind"></a>

Sector 22D is a YEIDA-planned group housing sector: roads, water, sewer and power are
laid by the authority to a master plan rather than left to individual projects.
Northwind's plot was allotted in the same 2026 auction cycle that brought several
established developers to the sector, so the neighbourhood will build out together —
sector roads, retail plots and institutional sites included — rather than around a
stand-alone tower cluster. An authority auction also means a clean title and a
development timeline set by YEIDA.

## Checklist Before Booking at Launch

<a id="checklist"></a>

- Buy with a **five-year horizon** — the corridor's payoff is 2028–31, not next quarter.
- **Check the RERA registration** before paying beyond an expression of interest; the number will be published on this site the moment it is granted.
- **Compare carpet areas**, not super areas, across projects — ask for both figures.
- Read the **furnishing schedule** annexed to the builder-buyer agreement.
- Ask for the **current price sheet** — founder inventory at the ₹8,500 all-inclusive rate is limited, and the standard list carries PLC and additional charges.
- Visit **NorthWind Sanctuary** in Sector Pi-1 on the same trip to judge the developer's finish quality first-hand.
