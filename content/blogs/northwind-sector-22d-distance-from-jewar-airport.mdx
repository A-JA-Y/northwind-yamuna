## The Route: Sector Road → Yamuna Expressway → Airport Approach

<a id="route"></a>

From the Northwind site you take the YEIDA sector road onto the Yamuna Expressway
service road, join the six-lane, access-controlled expressway southbound, pass the
Jewar toll plaza in about 8 minutes, and exit onto the airport approach road. There
are no railway crossings, no market stretches and no urban choke points on the way —
which is why the drive time holds up better than most "minutes from the airport"
claims in the NCR.

<a id="airport"></a>

## What Airport Proximity Does to Rents and Resale

<a id="rents"></a>

The pattern from other Indian airport corridors is consistent: once flights begin,
rental demand arrives first — airline crew, ground staff, hotel and logistics
employees — and end-user demand follows as the social infrastructure fills in.
Furnished homes rent faster and at a premium in this phase, because corporate and
crew tenants rarely furnish. Resale liquidity improves with each phase of airport
expansion, since every capacity increase adds employment within the same radius.

## Noise and Flight-Path Questions, Answered

<a id="noise"></a>

<Callout type="info">
  Sector 22D sits to the north-west of the airport, lateral to the current
  north-south runway alignment rather than under the primary approach funnel. At
  10–15 km lateral distance, aircraft noise at ground level is generally comparable
  to normal urban background sound. Buyers who are sensitive to this should visit the
  site at different hours — the honest way to judge it.
</Callout>
