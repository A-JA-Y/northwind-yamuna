## Rent and Price on Yamuna Expressway

**Rent.** A flat for rent on Yamuna Expressway is cheap for its size. In Sector 22D, 3 BHKs of 1,350 to 1,400 sq. ft. are listed at ₹16,000 to ₹22,000 a month. In Gaur Yamuna City, a similar 3 BHK asks about ₹25,000. This page uses ₹25,000, the top of that range, because the Northwind homes come furnished.

**Price.** The corridor's average apartment rate went from about ₹3,950 per sq. ft. in 2020 to about ₹10,200 in 2025. Northwind's launch rate is ₹8,500 per sq. ft.\*, with furnishing included.

**Yield.** At ₹25,000 a month, a year's rent is about 2.4% of ₹1.25 crore. At ₹20,000 it is under 2%. Property near Noida International Airport is priced on what the area may become, not on what it rents for today.

## How the 20×5 Payment Plan Changes the Sum

The 20×5 payment plan splits the price into five equal parts: booking, ground floor, 15th-floor roof, superstructure and offer of possession.

  ownShare={25}

  ]}
  caption="Where the money comes from on the worked example: your own 25% first, the 75% loan drawn late. GST, stamp duty and registration are on top."
/>

- **Your own 25% goes in first.** It covers the booking payment and part of the second.
- **The loan is drawn late.** Most of it goes out in the last three stages, so interest during construction is lower than on a loan drawn at the start. On this example it comes to about ₹13 lakh over five years.
- **You keep renting meanwhile.** At ₹25,000 a month rising 5% a year, that is about ₹16.6 lakh over five years.

## When Does Buying Break Even?

Owning costs more in cash, so it wins only if the flat gains value. Suppose a tenant invests everything the buyer pays out beyond rent. How fast must the flat appreciate for the buyer to end up level?

How likely is that? The corridor's average rate rose about 158% between 2020 and 2025, which is roughly 21% a year. That was the run-up to the airport, and the airport is now open. One consultant's forecast for the next two years works out to about 11% a year. Both are above the bar, and neither is a promise. A market that has already tripled can also pause.

## Tax on Rent and Home Loans

- **Old regime:** the owner of a self-occupied home can deduct up to ₹2 lakh of interest a year. A salaried tenant can claim HRA relief.
- **New regime:** neither is available.
- **Under construction:** interest paid before possession is claimed later, in five yearly parts.

Check your own position with a tax adviser.

## Conclusion

On rent vs buy house on Yamuna Expressway, the monthly sum is one-sided: rent is about a third of the EMI. Buying here is a decision about growth, and it needs a little over 7% a year for a decade to pay off. If you need a home near the airport now, rent. If you can hold for ten years, carry the EMI comfortably and believe in the corridor, buying is a reasonable bet. Put your own numbers in before you decide.
