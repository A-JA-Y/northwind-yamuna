## What 20×5 Means

<a id="meaning"></a>

You pay **20% of the cost at booking/allotment** and the **balance in 5% instalments**
tied to construction milestones — excavation, plinth, successive slab casts, finishing
stages — with the final balance at offer of possession. The exact milestone schedule
is part of the price sheet and will be annexed to the builder-buyer agreement.

## How Home Loans Disburse Against Construction-Linked Plans

<a id="loans"></a>

Banks and HFCs disburse in tranches that mirror the developer's demand letters at
each milestone. Until possession you pay **pre-EMI interest** on the amount disbursed
so far (or full EMI, if you opt for it). Sanction letters are issued once the project
holds a UP RERA registration; pre-approvals based on your income documents can start
earlier, so you are ready to draw down when registration is granted. On a ₹1.23 Cr*
home with 80% funding, a ₹98 lakh loan at 8.5% over 20 years works out to an EMI of
roughly ₹85,000 a month.

## Questions to Ask Before Signing the Payment Schedule

<a id="questions"></a>

- Is the **milestone list objective** (slab counts) or vague ("on commencement of finishing")?
- What is the **interest rate on delayed instalments** — and is the developer's delay-compensation rate symmetrical?
- Are **GST, IFMS and PLC** shown separately on the cost sheet, or bundled?
- Does the booking amount remain an **expression of interest with refundable terms** until UP RERA registration is granted?
- Is the **furnishing schedule** referenced in the agreement, since the price includes it?
