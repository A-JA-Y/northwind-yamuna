NorthWind Estates has emerged as the highest bidder in a Yamuna Expressway Industrial
Development Authority (YEIDA) auction, securing a 5-acre group housing land parcel in
Sector 22D with a winning bid of ₹189 crore. The auction marked renewed momentum in
YEIDA's group housing segment after a prolonged gap, with increased participation from
established developers across the NCR.

Sector 22D is rapidly emerging as one of the fastest-growing sectors in the YEIDA
region because of its proximity to Noida International Airport — which began
commercial flights on 15 June 2026 — and to the 1,000-acre International Film City
coming up in the adjoining Sector 21.

<NewsPullQuote
  quote="The Yamuna Expressway region is entering a completely new phase of urban and economic transformation. Infrastructure of the airport's scale creates long-term ecosystems rather than short-term real estate growth — which is why we chose to commit to the corridor early."

For buyers, an authority allotment through open auction carries practical weight: a
clean, authority-allotted title, a defined plot, and a development timeline set by
YEIDA — three factors that reduce risk at launch stage.

The developer's ongoing flagship, NorthWind Sanctuary in Sector Pi-1, Greater Noida —
4.5 acres, 185 residences, IGBC Platinum pre-certified — is built on the same
low-density principles, and serves as the closest preview of what Sector 22D will
deliver. Possession at Sector 22D is tentatively planned for
2030–31*, to be confirmed with the UP RERA registration.
