Noida International Airport (IATA: DXN) opened to commercial flights on 15 June 2026,
turning years of announcements into an operating fact on the ground. Phase 1 runs a
single 3,900-metre runway with a 1,01,590 sq. m. terminal and capacity for 12 million
passengers a year; the master plan scales the airport to 70 million passengers a year
by Phase 4. It is operated by Yamuna International Airport Pvt. Ltd., a wholly owned
subsidiary of Zurich Airport International AG.

For the YEIDA residential belt, the operational date matters more than any master
plan. Airports of this scale concentrate hotel, MRO, cargo and logistics employment
within a 15–20 km radius, and the housing demand that follows arrives rental-first —
crew, ground staff and corporate tenants who want furnished homes close to their
shifts.
